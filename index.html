The Blueprint

The simplest training blueprint on earth.

2 sessions a week. 6 exercises total. 1 heavy set each. For the rest of your life.

The two sessions

Alternate Session A and Session B. Minimum 48 hours between sessions. Two or three exercises each — pick what you have time for.

Session A
Push · Pull · Press
  1. 01 Dips
  2. 02 Chin-ups
  3. 03 Overhead Press optional 3rd
Session B
Squat · Hinge · Row
  1. 01 Squats
  2. 02 Romanian Deadlift
  3. 03 Bent-over Row optional 3rd

The rules

  • Rep range
    5–10 reps per set.
  • Progression
    Hit 10 clean reps → add weight next session.
  • Working sets
    1 heavy set per exercise. Warm up as needed.
  • Failure
    Take every working set to failure — the point where the next rep cannot be completed with clean form.
  • Weekly cap
    Max 6 failure sets per week. More is not better.
  • Form
    Clean form before weight. Always. No exceptions.

The equipment

Everything you need, forever. Nothing more.

  • Power rack
  • Olympic barbell
  • Flat bench
  • Dip & chin-up belt — for adding plates once bodyweight gets easy
  • Safety gear — collars, pins, wrist and knee support
  • Free weights up to 200 kg

That's it. No programs to buy. No apps. No cycles.
Train hard twice a week. Add weight when you can. Do it for the next 30 years.
